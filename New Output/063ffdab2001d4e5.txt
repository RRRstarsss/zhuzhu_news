For a third day in a row, President Trump took to Twitter to insist that he approved of White House counsel Don McGahn giving hours of testimony in the FBI’s Russia investigation.

Here & Now‘s Robin Young talks with NBC senior politics editor Beth Fouhy (@bfouhy) about what we know about the testimony, and how Trump’s tweets are reverberating.
