Former Equifax CEO testifies before Congress

Published: 2017-10-03T19:46:20Z

The former chairman and CEO of Equifax is testifying before Congress Tuesday about the massive data breach that's now affecting 145.5 million Americans.

Richard Smith apologized for what happened and blamed human error.

Lawmakers are also expected to question smith on why it took the credit reporting agency several months to notify consumers about the incident.
